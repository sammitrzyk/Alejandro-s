Standard section opener: eyebrow, serif H2, optional lead.

```jsx
<SectionHead eyebrow="Where we work" title="Twenty miles out of New Cumberland." intro="Six counties, and we know how each township runs." />
```

Titles are capped at 18ch so they break into two or three short lines like a headline, not a sentence. Left-aligned unless the section is a full-width CTA band.
