Primary action control — use for every estimate request, phone link and page-level CTA.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" size="lg" href="/estimate">Request an estimate</Button>
<Button variant="inverse" size="lg" href="tel:7175550142">Call (717) 555-0142</Button>
```

Variants: `primary` (green fill, the default), `accent` (gold fill — **at most one per screen**, reserved for the single most important action on the page), `outline` (hairline, light grounds), `inverse` (hairline, dark or photographic grounds), `solidLight` (white fill on dark grounds).

Pair rule: an estimate CTA and a phone CTA always appear together. Primary/accent on the left, outline/inverse on the right. Never centre the pair — left-align it under the copy.

Corners are square by design. Do not add border-radius.
