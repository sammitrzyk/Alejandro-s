Labelled form control. Square corners, uppercase micro-label above, gold focus ring.

```jsx
<Field label="Phone" name="phone" type="tel" required hint="We call back the same day, most days." />
<Field label="What are you looking at?" name="project" type="select" options={["Paver patio","Retaining wall","Walkway","Landscaping","Snow removal","Something else"]} />
```

Use `tone="dark"` inside dark CTA bands. Keep forms to five fields or fewer — the project-type select does more qualifying work than three extra text inputs.
