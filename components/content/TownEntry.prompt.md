One town on the service-area page.

```jsx
<TownEntry town="Camp Hill" county="Cumberland County" distance="3 mi"
  image="assets/photos/stone-edge-bed.jpg" alt="Stone-edged planting bed"
  notes="Older lots off Market Street sit on shale close to the surface, so wall footings get cut rather than dug. The borough issues its own permit. Most rear yards fall toward the house, which puts drainage ahead of layout." />
```

**The `notes` prop is the entire point of this component.** Two to four short declarative sentences of operational fact — permit office, soil, slope, frost depth, HOA architectural review, access constraints. If you cannot write real notes for a town, that town does not get its own page; fold it into a county cluster page instead.

Copy voice: Costa Custom Pools. Short sentences, no adjectives, no selling.
