Draggable before/after slider.

```jsx
<BeforeAfter before="assets/photos/patio-before-concrete.jpg" after="assets/photos/patio-pergola-mulch.jpg"
  beforeAlt="Plain concrete slab" afterAlt="Finished patio with pergola and fresh beds"
  caption="Slab replaced, beds re-cut, lawn regraded. Mechanicsburg." />
```

Use **only** where the two photographs were taken from roughly the same position. A mismatched pair reads as a trick and costs more trust than it buys. One per page is plenty.
