A single review, set large in the display serif and placed **in context** — beside the service it refers to, not corralled in a testimonials section.

```jsx
<ReviewQuote size="lg" tone="dark" quote="They cut the whole slope out by hand where the machine couldn't reach."
  name="Dana R." town="Camp Hill, PA" service="Retaining wall" />
```

No star graphics, no cards, no carousel. If an aggregate rating is shown anywhere, it must appear with its review count and both must be real.

Two or three reviews per page, placed where they answer a specific doubt. Twenty reviews in a row is noise.
