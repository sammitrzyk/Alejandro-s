# Service areas — structure and owner checklist (9 Oct 2026)

## Pages
- `/service-areas/` — H1 "Landscaping & Hardscaping Service Areas". Coverage list by drive-time ring, links to town pages, completed-project strip, estimate form.
- `/service-areas/new-cumberland-pa/` — base town. Projects: driveway & walkway, walkway & steps; cards: Bridge Street Dental, Family Physician Associates, two landscaping cards.
- `/service-areas/mechanicsburg-pa/` — projects: pool patio rebuild, patio/wall/fire pit; cards: seat wall & fire pit, river rock, bed edging.
- `/service-areas/camp-hill-pa/` — cards only: foundation bed & flagstone edge, island bed & fieldstone edging.

Each town page: title, meta, canonical, breadcrumb (visible + JSON-LD), H1 "Landscaping & Hardscaping in [Town], PA", opening statement, completed projects in that town, services linked to service pages, 4 coverage FAQs, estimate form. No office implied outside New Cumberland.

Links: service pages with related projects show a "Where we work" line linking only to towns with a project for that service, plus All service areas; project pages with a verified town link to their town page; town pages link to each project and service; Service Areas links to all town pages.
