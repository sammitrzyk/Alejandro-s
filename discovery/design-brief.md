Owner sent URL, no comment.

APPLY: Central PA equivalents are real and knowable — West Shore clay and slope vs flatter Lancaster ground; frost depth and base spec for PA freeze-thaw; township permit thresholds for retaining wall height (many PA townships require engineered drawings above 4 ft); HOA architectural review in Hershey/Hummelstown developments; steep-lot drainage on the Camp Hill / New Cumberland ridge. This is the single highest-value content investment on the whole site.
